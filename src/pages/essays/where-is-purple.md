# Where is Purple?

Life would be so much easier if I knew what I wanted, right?

The good thing about being Eve, in the garden of Eden, is that there is only one thing to want and that is the Apple.

But alas,

Copernicus proved we were not the center of the universe, 
Darwin proved we were not the center of life,
and thus Freud proved we were not even the center of our own consciousness

much to the contrary: like Eve, we don’t want what we desire. Eve did not care for the Apple, she cared for knowledge, out of all things. She cared for the novel, the unexplored, the what if. 

And for that, an Apple could never satisfy.

“Reality is, by nature, unsatisfactory” said the Buddhists. Who without realizing had just answered a question no one thought to ask: 

where is purple?

for so much of human history, there was no such thing as “purple dye” or “purple paint”

there was only a faint perception a die hard reminiscence for a time that never came 

a time of wealth and growth and so many wonderful things that were yet to come 

for it was so expensive to taint things purple.

But now purple is everywhere. It’s on the streets, it’s in your house, it’s under that tree, it’s up your nose
there is no escaping this bonanza of material wealth and yet we have so, so little still.

"Reality is, by nature, unsatisfactory", said the buddhists.

yes of course! It’s unsatisfactory because if I eat the amount of cake I want to eat, I’ll die before I take the last bite.
What’s the key insight on cake? What are the top five action items for this piece of bread?

I hope I don’t have to dye things purple anymore. I am so, so tired. 

But it’s not all over. Over and over and over again, the writing’s on the wall: we are **not** cooked. 

Other apples will grow, more asymmetry will appear. More love, and conflict, and pressure gradients and temperature for us all. 

Let us not forget of what is most important:

That we do not know what we want.
